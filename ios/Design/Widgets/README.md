# Widget and motion revision

Three widget designs share the same dated observation: Instrument, Garage and OBC. Garage and OBC provide small, medium and large sizes; all three provide the small size for StandBy and CarPlay. Each keeps units, missing-channel handling, sample age and source-separated storage.

Large Garage gives the car the full width above all five readings. Large OBC emphasizes the selected channel with four secondary readings below. Both use `systemLarge`, the largest iPhone Home Screen widget family supported by the current Xcode 26.2 toolchain. These sizes do not change the smaller CarPlay presentation.

The Garage image comes from the shipped Metal renderer and the app E36 mesh, with the same studio lighting and shaders. The app runs this scene in 3D; widgets use a transparent bitmap. No photograph cutout or substitute car is used. The image retains its full-color rendering in tinted widget appearances. Paint designation: **295 · Samoablau Metallic**.

To regenerate the bitmap, build the Debug simulator app and launch with `--demo --vehicle-review=widget --scene-study=graphite --widget-car-export`. After warm-up it writes `Documents/widget-car.png` in the simulator app container. Save that file, then repeat with `--widget-car-only` to capture the same camera without the shadow receiver. Run `python3 ios/Design/Widgets/prepare-car.py full-scene.png vehicle-only.png ios/WidgetShared/Assets.xcassets/WidgetCar.imageset/car.png`. The script isolates and feathers the shadow contribution while preserving the vehicle, and asserts completely transparent image edges. Cropping the original long studio shadow directly leaves the visible rectangle reported on the Home Screen. `car-render.json` records the adopted sources and crop. Both export flags are Debug-only and never change the production display transform. Use them only in the simulator.

In normal tabs, a single tap advances through exterior angles. Expanded mode provides horizontal/vertical orbit, unrestricted practical dolly and screen-plane panning, with simultaneous two-finger pan/pinch. Camera buttons and reset animate focus and distance back to a fitted view. Closing expanded mode removes its translation and distance from the dashboard camera; the same renderer survives. Camera buttons use the shortest orbit; tabs use their ordered quarter-turns. The camera moves around the stationary car and lights. There is no idle animation, no motion driven by telemetry, and no rendering while Instruments, the connection sheet or the app background hides the scene. Reduce Motion makes view changes immediate and disables coast.

The supplied BMW roundel is also the iPhone and Watch app icon. DEMO badges and notification/export prefixes have been removed; simulation remains an explicit selectable data source and keeps its own storage.
