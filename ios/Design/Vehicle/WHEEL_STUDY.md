# Wheel study — 10 September 2026

[WheelStudy.blend](WheelStudy.blend) is a standalone, packed Blender study of the owner's 18-spoke wheel, with [a neutral studio preview](wheel-study.png). The owner asked to retain this revision and continue the project, while noting that its shape still differs from the reference.

The reference photographs show `7Jx15 IS47` and `BMW 1180069-12`. The study has a nominal 7-inch barrel, 47 mm mounting offset, five stepped bolt pockets and five smaller interleaved holes. Photographic proportions are approximations, not a scan.

The centre-cap artwork starts with the owner's supplied [BMW SVG](ReferenceLogos/bmw-logo.svg). The vehicle and this study adapt its letter sizes, blue quarters and chrome separators to the later badge/cap photographs; the derived vectors are in `ReferenceLogos/`. The raster and material masks are packed into the Blender file; the artwork is applied to the domed enamel geometry.

The current spoke construction has a rounded raised crown, straight sides and a hollow back, with flared roots. It remains a visual approximation of the intended U profile. This revision supplies the wheels for the release vehicle and its app views; the standalone study preserves the same geometry for future editing.

The complete candidate vehicle and reproducible iteration scripts remain in `.context/vehicle-3d/corrections-sep10/`; `wheel-v10-offset.blend` records the retained wheel revision. The delivered study is refreshed from the final vehicle, including its cap artwork and removal of five overlapping hub triangles without moving vertices. See that directory's `STATUS.md` for iteration history. The later instruction to continue supersedes its earlier pending-clarification notes.
