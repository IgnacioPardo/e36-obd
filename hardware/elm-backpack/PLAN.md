# Inline ELM327 ECU adapter — revision E

Make a black printed enclosure for the user's loose buck converter and ESP32-S3, alongside the existing ELM BT shell. Keep the BMW-and-stripes detail; remove the broad, strapped-over desktop-box proportions. Preserve direct soldering and USB pointing away from the OBD plug.

The revised body is **162 × 48 × 30.4 mm**: boards end-to-end, mounting below the electronics, screws from underneath, uninterrupted long cover. Relative to D, width falls 41.5%, length increases 52 mm, footprint area falls 13.8%, and height stays unchanged. This trades width for a longer tail that needs available vehicle space and a suitable support.

This is a dimensioned first-fit prototype. No physical measurements have been provided. The ELM and electronics remain marked **FIT CHECK REQUIRED**. The old `hardware/case.py` targets a different custom PCB and is not reused.

## Layout and mechanical decisions

1. **End-to-end boards.** Buck centre (0,-32); ESP overall-envelope centre (0,45.5). Their nominal outlines occupy y=-63.75…-0.25 and y=13…78, leaving 13.25 mm between them. Converter terminal-wire allowance extends to y=11.95; the ESP antenna begins at 13. Both assemblies remain accessible with the lid off. Stacking would concentrate heat and complicate service; it was not selected.
2. **Height follows the converter.** Inner floor z=2.4; buck underside=6.4; PCB top=8; highest provisional component=26; lid underside=28. The 2 mm roof clearance gives 30.4 mm overall. Removing the ESP headers reduces its requirements, but cannot reduce the converter's height. The 18 mm buck component allowance needs measurement before reducing it.
3. **Padded buck edge clamps.** Four supports and M3 × 6 keepers at x=±17.45, y=-43.43/-20.57. The lower and upper pads contact only the outer 1 mm of PCB. Buck solder allowance=3, leaving 1 mm to the floor or flush mounting strap. Recheck all four actual contact patches before printing the base.
4. **Headerless ESP.** PCB underside=7.4, solder allowance=1.5, 3.5 mm left for insulated wire routing. Two padded central seats avoid both pin rows; side guides and a sleeved tie over the RF shield retain the board. No clamp or tie loads the antenna. Both USB mouths point toward +Y, with a 32 × 8.5 shared tail window at y=81.
5. **Wire entry between the boards.** A 16 × 12 rounded floor opening at (-9,6) sits beyond the reference ELM tail. The 6 mm-wide strain-relief anchor at (11,6) is clear of the opening and beneath the reserved terminal-wire turn. Existing ELM-hole dimensions remain unknown. Route insulated wires below the ESP and leave service slack back to the hole.
6. **Straps below the converter.** Two 10 mm-wide, approximately 1 mm-thick straps cross recessed inner-floor lanes at y=-55/-9.3. Floor slots are 2.4 × 12 at x=±18.6. Each lane leaves 1.4 mm floor skin, with the strap sitting flush to the normal 2.4 mm floor. Slots and channels avoid buck supports and end stops. The loops flare outward in the 3 mm mounting-pad gap and wrap beneath the ELM; they do not cross the lid. Reference loop maximum width is 52.4 mm around a 50 mm ELM; body width alone is 48 mm.
7. **Extended tail support.** The nominal ELM body occupies y=-81…-3, leaving an 84 mm extension. A third recessed slot pair at y=54 accepts a separate tie to a suitable existing support. It is positioned clear of ESP seats and guides. Its vehicle-side route is not modeled because no mounting-point dimensions are known. Verify plug, loom, USB cable and installation clearance physically.
8. **Lid screws from underneath.** Four M3 × 25 screws at x=±19,y=±75.6 pass through 3.4 mm base clearance holes. Base posts stop at z=20.2; lid bosses start at z=20.5. Boss radius=2.8, 2.6 mm pilot, 6 mm blind depth; nominal screw engagement=4.5. Base corner relief radius=3.15 gives 0.35 radial clearance. Lid locating rim depth=2, per-side clearance=0.35. Heads ≤2 mm fit within 3 mm pads. Detach from ELM for screw access. Inverted lid print height is 9.9; bosses occupy interior space when assembled.
9. **Long BMW ribbed cover.** A 136 × 25 field, recessed 0.4, runs along Y. Nine 1.25 mm ribs at 2.5 mm pitch frame a 30 mm BMW wordmark. Forty-eight 12 × 1.25 segmented lid openings leave 4 mm solid braces between sections; their clipped corners yield about 716 mm² open area. Fifteen 2 × 8 side vents per wall over the buck add 480 mm² and retain the top 2 mm rim. Black print; optional silver highlights only on BMW and rib faces. No extra caption or cosmetic side panels.
10. **Dry-location prototype.** Ventilation is not sealing or a thermal rating. Verify the actual closed assembly's temperature and Wi-Fi/BLE operation. This work packages the existing circuit; it does not qualify the circuit or enclosure for a specific automotive environmental standard.

## Fabrication and verification

- Deliver parametric source, individual millimetre STL/STEP parts, reference assembly STEP, printable-only 3MF, offline viewer, actual-mesh renders, dimension sheet and assembly instructions.
- Print the inexpensive board-outline/screw-hole gauge first. Measure actual board length/width, carrier ends, antenna/USB overhang, capacitor height and solder joints. A flat gauge does not establish heights, pad contact or wire bends.
- Verify one valid solid per printed part, zero printed-part and provisional packaging-envelope clashes, closed consistent single-component meshes with positive volume, and z=0 print orientation. Include straps-to-printed-parts, ESP retaining-tie clearance, buck-to-ESP envelopes and straps-to-ELM reference checks.
- Reopen the 3MF to check units and placements. Approximate required layout space is 110 × 182 mm before brim. Do not include printer-specific settings or claim machine slicing.
- Print base floor down; lid face down, rim and bosses up; keepers flat. Start at 0.4 nozzle / 0.20 layers / 4 walls / 5 top and bottom layers / 30% gyroid. Inspect the short lid-field bridges and 2 mm side-vent roofs. The 32 mm USB-window roof needs local removable support. Floor strap recesses are open upward.
- Prefer unfilled ASA for the intended installation, following its manufacturer and [Prusa's ASA guidance](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/asa_1809); use PETG for a cool bench fit check if needed. Thermal suitability depends on measured installation conditions.
- Assemble unpowered, checking pads, clamps, screw engagement, protected wire entry, insulated solder joints, cable slack, USB mating and mounting stability. Run the intended load plus RF checks with the lid closed before final installation.
